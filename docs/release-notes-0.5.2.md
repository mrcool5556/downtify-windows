**Mix your own style.** The Theme page now lets you pick each part of the look on its own: button style (Flat, Lipped, Raised, Inset, Neon, Cut paper, 90s bevel), fonts, corners, window surfaces, the lip and its shadow, text fields and switches. Every setting works with every other.
- **Presets** set the whole look in one click (Downtify, Studio, Gold arcade, Paper desk, Retro 95, Synth night). Hover one and click ✕ to hide it; **Restore default presets** brings them back.
- **Save your own mixes** and switch between them.
- **Advanced** picks a button style for each player part (play, skip, toggles and so on) and a player key shape.
- New colour themes: **Studio Dark** and **Machined Light**. Sleek is now called **Machined**.
- The colour themes sit in two columns, **☾ Dark** and **☀ Light**.

**Two looks, light and dark.** The sun/moon button now swaps between two complete looks, not just the colours. Each side keeps its own theme, buttons and style, so your dark look and your light look can be completely different. Clicking a theme from the other column switches look the same way.

**Glass themes** get a lit backdrop behind the frosted cards, so the glass reads in any theme.

**Mini player:**
- Its own fonts, corners and lip, a **Copy a look** button, and window looks: Soft inset, Indented, Bevel and **Recessed**, which sits pressed into your desktop. An inset depth slider sets how deep.
- When see-through, its text and buttons get a crisp outline, so they stay readable over white or busy wallpapers and games.

**Taskbar buttons.** Hover Downtify on the taskbar and the preview shows **Back, Play/Pause and Forward**.

**Liking big lists is much faster.** Dropping songs on Liked Songs likes 8 at a time instead of one by one. The seek bar shows the progress while it runs: liked so far on the left, the total on the right.

**Smaller fixes:**
- Lyrics page: the song and artist's details are on the left, with a round ✕ to close. Going anywhere else (a playlist, the artist, the song name) now closes the lyrics too.
- While a song loads, the seek bar shimmers in your theme colour instead of showing a Loading bubble.
- Notes such as "Added 12 to…" float just above the player bar, so you see them however far you've scrolled.
- Hovering Liked Songs says you can drop songs there to like them, instead of "not editable".

Updating from 0.5.1: Settings → About → Update. Unsigned installer: Windows SmartScreen may warn on a fresh install.
