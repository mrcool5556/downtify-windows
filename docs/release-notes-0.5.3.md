**The song list follows the music.** When the song changes (Next, Back, or a song ending), the list glides so the new song sits in the middle, the way the queue does. With shuffle on, it glides to wherever the next song is.
- Scrolled away to browse? The list stays where you are, and starts following again once the playing song is back on screen.
- A song you double-click stays put under your mouse.

**Jump to the playing song.** Song lists have the queue's **⌖** button next to the view buttons: one click brings the playing song back to the middle. It's greyed out when the playing song isn't in the list.

Updating from 0.5.2: Settings → About → Update. Unsigned installer: Windows SmartScreen may warn on a fresh install.
