Second preview of the Windows client for a self-hosted Downtify server. Everything from 0.1.0 plus:

**Mini player** (Ctrl+M or the button at the right of the player bar): a small always-on-top bar with playback, shuffle, repeat, heart, download, volume, an up-next drawer, and adjustable opacity (10 to 100 %) that can fade only the background so the song stays readable over whatever is behind it. Pin, minimize, restore and close live in its top-right corner.

**Playlist sharing** (needs Downtify 3.7.0 or newer): share a playlist privately with named people or publicly with everyone on the server; see "Shared with me" and "Public playlists" in the sidebar; follow public ones; save a copy of any you can open; detach an imported playlist to make it editable.

**Playlist editing**: create, rename, delete; add and remove songs from right-click menus; multi-select with Shift and Ctrl; drag songs onto a playlist (Shift to move). Needs a one-time sign-in under Settings → Server.

**New shell**: frameless window with its own controls; top bar with back/forward, one search box that also takes a Spotify or YouTube Music link to download, shortcuts overlay, light and dark themes; collapsible sidebar with Home, Downloads, your playlists, server status, Offline mode and Settings; Settings page with sections and an update check.

**Tray**: tray icon with play/pause, next, previous, show and quit; closing the window keeps the music playing; optional start with Windows.

**Player**: Windows media keys and media popup, right-click menus everywhere, queue panel with Queue and Recently played tabs, drag to reorder, play next and add to queue, artist pages, resizable Title and Artist columns, three list layouts.

**Downloads**: "Sync to this PC" on a playlist keeps it downloaded as songs are added; per-song download buttons with progress; Downloads view with what's missing greyed above what's on this PC; songs deleted on the server stay on your PC, marked.

Unsigned installer: Windows SmartScreen will warn on first run. Settings and the library index from 0.1.0 carry over.
