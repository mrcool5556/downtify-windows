**Themes.** Settings → Theme has eight colour themes from our design library (Downtify, Studio Light, Retro Console, Arcade, Night Glass, GR1MOS, Cozy, Lavender Glass) and two control styles: **Modern** (flat) or **Heavy** (raised keys in a metal bezel that press down). The sun/moon button switches between your last dark and light theme. Under **Advanced** you can set each part of the player to Modern or Heavy on its own, pick round or square buttons, choose a stronger highlight for selected songs and playlists, and hide shuffle/repeat, the volume slider, download or the heart.

**Mini player.**
- Gets narrower without losing the song: shuffle and repeat fold away first, then play and next move beside the heart, then previous and download go. Anything folded away is in the mini's right-click strip.
- Right-click opens a themed strip of keys that fits inside the mini (previous, shuffle, repeat, like, download, add to playlist, go to album, open full window).
- Drag the handle to size the up-next drawer to any height; click it to open or close.
- Opacity goes down to 0% and still comes back on hover; fade only the background or the whole window.
- Its own theme and style if you want; the cover and song name drag the window.

**Search.** Typing filters the list you're in; **Enter** searches songs, albums, artists and playlists across the whole library. Pasted links still download.

**Tray menu** shows the current song, Play or Pause, next/previous, like, shuffle, a repeat submenu, mini/full window, settings and quit, and keeps them in step with the player.

**Also:** drop songs on Liked Songs to like them all; shadow switches for the window and the mini; no accidental text highlighting while dragging; centred icons in every key; "Loading…" no longer makes the player bar jump; tidier Settings.

Updating from 0.3.0: Settings → About → Update. Unsigned installer: Windows SmartScreen may warn on a fresh install.
