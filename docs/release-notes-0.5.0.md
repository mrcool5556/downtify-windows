**Discover.** A new Discover page in the sidebar, built on your server's own suggestions:
- **New artists for you**, based on what you listen to. Right-click an artist for "Not interested" (only hides them for you).
- **Albums and playlists for you**. Open one to see its tracklist, play it, or press **Add all to library**.
- **Top charts.**
- Songs that aren't on your server can still be played: in full when the server can stream them, otherwise as a preview.

**Like this.** The compass button in the player bar opens a "Like this" tab in the queue panel with songs similar to the one playing. **Start radio** keeps the queue topped up with more like it.

**Online results in search.** Pressing Enter in search also shows matching songs from outside your library.

**The heart saves songs.** On a song from outside your library (Discover, online search, radio), the heart asks the server to download it and likes it once it arrives. The heart pulses while it waits; it works from the player bar, the mini player, the mini's strip, Ctrl+L and the tray.

**Sort any song list.** Click Title, Artist, Album or Time: once for A to Z, twice for Z to A, a third time for the list's own order. Playing from a sorted list plays in that order.

**Also:** the GR1MOS theme is now called **Sleek**; lists with the same song in them twice no longer show rows out of order; covers that fail to load in Discover and Like this show a placeholder instead of a broken image.

Updating from 0.4.0: Settings → About → Update. Unsigned installer: Windows SmartScreen may warn on a fresh install.
